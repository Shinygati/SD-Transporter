Copy the CONTENTS of compiled/SD/ (or SD/ in the update ZIP) onto your card.

Install android/Pokewalker-Android.apk over the current same-key Android app.
Update both console and phone companions. Read howtouse.txt before syncing.

This update includes:
- Deleted/empty profile rebinding and phone returns after all console walker
  files were deleted. From the console file list: Y, choose HGSS, A, confirm.
- Verified final-file return acknowledgement and recovery-only history.
- NDS direct-storage alignment, with 10 recovery copies retained per walker.
- Original-style phone controls, uploaded course scene and live LCD counters.
- The supplied device shell, including both original reflections.
- Same-phone daily profile visits and nearby-phone Bluetooth visits/battles.
- Friendly battles cost 30 watts each, use retained moves and save entry/reward
  receipts. Expanded rewards require both phones to opt in; mixed settings warn.
- Optional expanded Dowsing items and confirmed special/event course activation.

  email in either page.

Bluetooth is for phone visits and battles. Console handoffs remain local Wi-Fi.
Expanded item groups and exact rates are listed in howtouse.txt and README.md.
Friendly battles use simplified rules, separate from the HGSS battle engine.

Extensive automated testing has been performed. Use at your own risk and keep
independent save backups. Actual phone radios and physical console behavior
cannot be confirmed by the host checks. iPhone is SOURCE ONLY; producing an IPA
requires an Apple SDK build and signing. No retail ROMs, saves, personal walker
states, SDKs or private signing keys are included.
