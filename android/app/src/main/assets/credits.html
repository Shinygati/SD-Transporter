Pokewalker

A companion for the virtual Pokéwalker in SD Transporter. Developed by Shinygati.

Source and licence

The companion is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. Read the licence or visit the source repository for build instructions and complete notices.

Credits

SD Transporter's console features build on TWiLight Menu++ and nds-bootstrap.

Pokéwalker format and protocol research: Dmitry Grinberg, mamba2410 and contributors, and the lumirth/pw reconstruction.

Game-format references and factual species, item and move mappings: pret/pokeheartgold and PKHeX. Console builds use devkitPro libraries; their notices are included with the source.

Development assistance: OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude.

Compatibility

This independent project is not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company. Game names identify compatibility. Displayed game sprites, menus and sound scores come from your own Pokémon data. Retail ROMs, saves and firmware are not bundled. The device shell image was supplied by the project owner; its original sample LCD is covered by the live display. Image rights remain with their respective owners.
