## Full description

Walker Companion lets you continue a virtual walking adventure on your phone and return it to a compatible Nintendo DS or Nintendo 3DS homebrew companion over local Wi-Fi.

Choose your phone's step counter or an optional time-based walking mode. Earn watts, explore routes, search moving grass in Radar, find items through Dowsing, and manage the Pokémon and items collected on your walk.

Transfer ownership to one device at a time. While the phone holds the walker, the console copy stays locked. Returning the walker unlocks it on the console. Recovery copies help preserve earlier data if a transfer is interrupted or you need to recover a session.

Features include:

- Physical step counting on supported phones, with optional simulated walking.
- Radar encounters, battles, catching, Dowsing and carried-item management.
- Local Wi-Fi sync with compatible DS and 3DS companion software.
- Saved console IP addresses and exportable local recovery copies.
- No accounts, advertising or developer-operated cloud sync.

A compatible console companion and your own valid walker data are required. No game ROMs or retail game assets are included. Physical step tracking requires a supported step-counter sensor and your permission. Nintendo DS Lite networking requires compatible legacy Wi-Fi equipment.

This is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures or The Pokémon Company.
