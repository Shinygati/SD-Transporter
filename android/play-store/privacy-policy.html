Privacy

Pokewalker stores your profiles and progress on your device. It has no advertising, analytics, developer accounts or cloud service.

Stored information

Profiles contain Pokémon and trainer information, course data, steps, watts and rewards. The app also saves your settings, console addresses, recovery copies and the records used to prevent conflicting transfers or repeated rewards.

Steps

Physical step counting uses the phone's activity sensor. All active profiles can earn progress from those steps. Android may run an ongoing notification while counting; iPhone uses the motion history provided by iOS. Time-based counting is optional and is disabled by default. You can pause either source in Settings.

Console transfers

When you choose Receive Pokémon or Return Pokémon, the app sends data directly to the DS or 3DS console address you entered. Transfers use HTTP on your local Wi-Fi network and are not encrypted. Use a private network you trust. No developer server receives the data.

Nearby phones

Bluetooth is used only when you open nearby-phone visits or battles. Connected phones exchange the trainer, Pokémon, move, sprite and step information needed for that activity. Visits and friendly battles do not transfer ownership of either Pokémon. Bluetooth discovery stops when you leave the connection screen.

Recovery and deletion

The app keeps up to ten recovery copies per Pokéwalker. A new copy is verified before the oldest is removed. Recovery copies are separate from active profiles and are not loaded automatically.

After a confirmed return, the active Pokémon is removed from this phone. An unconfirmed return remains locked for retry. Deleting an active profile's Pokémon preserves a recovery copy first and releases the profile's trainer pairing. Completed and deleted transfer records remain in private storage to prevent an older file from reactivating the same session.

You can export or delete recovery copies, delete active Pokémon, remove saved console addresses, and revoke permissions in your phone's settings. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes private profiles and recovery copies; files you exported or kept on a console remain separate. Return or export a Pokémon before clearing app data.

Permissions

Activity permissions support physical steps. Android notification and foreground-service permissions support walking while the screen is off. Bluetooth permissions support nearby-phone visits and battles. On older Android versions, Bluetooth discovery also requires location permission; the app does not request or store your geographic location.
