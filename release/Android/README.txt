Android Pokewalker app
======================

The app lets you take your Pokewalker out on your phone. It counts real
steps, and you can bring the Pokemon back to your console afterwards
over Wi-Fi.


Installing
----------

1. Copy SD-Transporter-Pokewalker-1.6.3.apk to your phone, or download
   it on the phone directly.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to
   "install unknown apps". That's normal for apps that don't come from
   the Play Store.
3. Tap Install.

If you already have the app, this updates it and keeps your walkers.


Before you update
-----------------

If your Pokemon is on the phone right now, send it back to the console
before updating, just to be safe. Never uninstall the app while it's
holding your Pokemon. Uninstalling deletes everything the app has saved.


"App not installed" or "conflicts with an existing package"
-----------------------------------------------------------

That means the copy already on your phone was signed with a different
key, usually a test build from before 4 November 2026. Android won't
update across keys. To switch:

  1. Return your Pokemon to the console first.
  2. In the app, export any recovery copies you want to keep.
  3. Uninstall the old app, then install this one.
  4. Receive your Pokemon from the console again.
