How to use the virtual Pokewalker
=================================

This works the same on every setup once everything is installed.


Turn it on for your game
------------------------

1. Put your HeartGold or SoulSilver ROM (.nds file) in the roms/nds
   folder on your card, if it isn't there already.

2. Turn on the console and open TWiLight Menu++.

3. Highlight HeartGold or SoulSilver (don't open it yet) and press Y.
   This opens that game's settings.

4. Find "Bootstrap:" and change it to "Nightly". This tells TWiLight to
   use the SD Transporter bootstrap instead of the normal one.

5. Find "Virtual Pokewalker: Off" and press A so it says
   "Virtual Pokewalker: On".

6. Press B to go back, then start the game as normal.


Sending a Pokemon on a stroll
-----------------------------

1. In the game, go to the Pokewalker option (on the title screen menu
   when you load your save).

2. Pick what you want to do, the same as you would with a real
   Pokewalker.

3. When the game says it's looking for the Pokewalker, press SELECT
   once. SELECT is your virtual Pokewalker's button, just like the button
   on a real one. A quick press is all it needs; you don't have to hold
   it. Press it again for each new connection.

4. When it's finished, save your game in the normal way.

IMPORTANT: do NOT hold SELECT and LEFT together while the game is
searching. Holding both for about two seconds wipes the virtual
Pokewalker back to a brand-new one, the same as deleting it. A quick
accidental press does nothing. If a Pokemon was out on a stroll when that
happens, close the game and start it again: SD Transporter notices and
puts the walker back.

The first time you connect, the game will register the walker to you,
just like a new real Pokewalker. Don't press anything or move the
console apart while it registers. If registering gets cut off and the
game later says "This Pokewalker has not been registered", close the
game and start it again: SD Transporter puts your registered walker
back. (A brand-new walker that was never registered just needs pairing
again from the game's Pokewalker menu.)


Walking, catching and finding items
-----------------------------------

1. Close the game (turn the console off and on, or go back to
   TWiLight Menu++).

2. Open "Pokewalker-NDS" from TWiLight Menu++. On a 3DS with homebrew
   you can open the 3DS Pokewalker app instead. It counts your real
   steps.

3. Choose your walker from the list. Your Pokemon starts walking.
   Steps build up while the app is open, and also while the console is
   closed or asleep in the app.

   If the walker is elsewhere, press X on the walker list. Use A to open a
   folder, B to go up, and START to search that folder and its subfolders.
   Press Y in the folder picker to search /roms again.

4. Use the Poke Radar to catch Pokemon and Dowsing to find items.

5. When you're done, press START. This saves and closes the app. Always
   leave with START, otherwise your progress isn't saved.

An empty walker still counts steps and watts, but you need to send a
Pokemon from the game to use the Radar and Dowsing.


Taking it out on your phone (optional)
--------------------------------------

The Android app (see the Android folder) can carry your walker around
and count real steps. Your console and phone need to be on the same
Wi-Fi network.

  1. In the Pokewalker-NDS companion, open your walker, go to Settings,
     then Phone sync. Leave this screen open.
  2. On the phone, open Console Wi-Fi sync, type in or pick the
     console's IP address (shown on the console screen), and choose
     Receive Pokemon from console.
  3. To bring it back later, open Phone sync on the console again and
     choose Return Pokemon on the phone.

While the phone has your Pokemon, the game and the console companion
can't use that walker. That's on purpose, so you can't end up with two
copies. Always return it to the console before playing HGSS.


Bringing the Pokemon home
-------------------------

Start the game, choose the Pokewalker option to return your Pokemon, and
press SELECT again when it searches. Anything you caught or found comes
across too. Save your game afterwards.


Where the walker is kept
------------------------

Each save gets its own walker file. It sits next to your save and has
the same name, ending in .walker instead of .sav. For example:

  roms/nds/saves/Pokemon HeartGold.sav
  roms/nds/saves/Pokemon HeartGold.walker

Copy that file somewhere safe now and then. Before every game launch,
SD Transporter also makes a backup in roms/nds/saves/transfers/backups.
If something ever goes wrong, the companion app can restore one of
those for you.


If it doesn't connect
---------------------

  - Check the game's settings again: "Bootstrap: Nightly" and
    "Virtual Pokewalker: On".
  - Make sure you press SELECT while the game is searching.
  - Look for _nds/nds-bootstrap/pokewalker-error.txt on your card. If
    it's there, it says why the walker wasn't connected.
  - If your Pokemon is out with the phone app, bring it back to the
    console first. The game can't see a walker the phone is holding.


"There is already a Pokemon on the Pokewalker"
----------------------------------------------

This happens when a send got onto the walker but the game didn't finish
saving, so the game still has your Pokemon in your party or PC. Close the
game and start it again from TWiLight Menu++. SD Transporter checks the
walker against your save every time the game starts, removes the leftover
copy, and you can send a Pokemon again. If it fixes anything, it writes a
note to _nds/nds-bootstrap/pokewalker-repair.txt.

It also works the other way: if your save says a Pokemon is out but the
walker has lost it, it puts the walker back from a saved copy.
