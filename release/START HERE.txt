SD Transporter v1.6.2
=====================

SD Transporter adds a virtual Pokewalker to Pokemon HeartGold and
SoulSilver, plus virtual Pal Park and Poke Transfer cartridges, through a
modified copy of TWiLight Menu++. Everything you need is in this folder,
including TWiLight Menu++ itself, so you don't have to download it
separately.


STEP 1: Work out which setup you have
-------------------------------------

Open only the ONE folder that matches you. Ignore the rest.

  "DS or DS Lite - flashcart"
      An original DS or DS Lite, with a flashcart (an R4, Acekard, DSTT,
      DSpico and so on) that you put a microSD card into.

  "DSi - flashcart"
      A DSi or DSi XL, using a flashcart. Pick this if you have NOT
      installed Unlaunch or hiyaCFW on the DSi itself.

  "3DS - flashcart"
      A 3DS, 2DS, New 3DS or New 2DS, using a flashcart in the cartridge
      slot.

  "DSi - custom firmware"
      A DSi or DSi XL that's been modded with Unlaunch (or hiyaCFW), so
      TWiLight Menu++ runs straight off the SD card in the side of the
      console.

  "3DS - custom firmware"
      A 3DS or 2DS with custom firmware (Luma3DS). If your 3DS shows
      "Luma3DS" when you hold SELECT while turning it on, this is you.

Not sure? If you play your games from a little card you stick into the
cartridge slot, you have a flashcart. If you've followed a guide like
dsi.cfw.guide or 3ds.hacks.guide, you have custom firmware.

There's also an "Android" folder for the phone app.


STEP 2: Back up first
---------------------

Before copying anything, make a copy of your whole SD card (or flashcart
microSD) on your computer. If you already use the Pokewalker, also keep a
copy of your .sav and .walker files. That way nothing can be lost.


STEP 3: Follow the README in your folder
----------------------------------------

Every setup folder has its own README.txt with step-by-step
instructions. The folders inside named "Copy to ..." tell you which card
their contents go on.

"Copy the contents" means: open the "Copy to ..." folder, select
everything inside it, and drag it onto the card. When your computer asks
whether to merge folders or replace files, say yes to all. Do NOT copy
the "Copy to ..." folder itself onto the card.


What's in here
--------------

  TWiLight Menu++ 27.25.1, with the SD Transporter changes already applied
  SD Transporter bootstrap (runs the games, and the virtual Pokewalker)
  Pokewalker companion for DS/DSi/3DS (walk, catch, find items)
  Real-cartridge Pokewalker launcher (DSi/3DS only, for retail HGSS carts)
  Pokewalker companion for 3DS (counts real steps)
  Android Pokewalker app

SHA256SUMS.txt lists a checksum for every file, if you want to check
nothing got damaged while downloading.
